# CERBERUS GENESIS X — Engineering requirements

Status: concept and architecture only. No functional manufactured handset.

1. Reference Android SoC development board with supported BSP, display and camera drivers.
2. Modular Wi-Fi/cellular radio configuration, battery protection and thermal design.
3. Satellite route must be evaluated with certified RF hardware, licensed network/provider, region, antenna, service subscription and local regulation. Satellite operation is not demonstrated.
4. Android interface may call MOSS services only through an authenticated, consent-based API.
5. Before physical construction: qualified electrical safety review, battery design, RF assessment, EMC, reliable written supplier quotes and test records.
6. Gate every claim with the requirements.json evidence list.
