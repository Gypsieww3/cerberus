CONCEPT / FUTURE PILOT
Our operating principle
“Ambition is the starting point. Evidence, engineering and collaboration turn it into technology.”

C.E.R.B.E.R.U.S. is an early-stage initiative founded in Curitiba, Brazil. Our current priority is turning experimental AI software into reproducible demonstrations and useful applications.

We distinguish working prototypes from engineering concepts and long-term research goals. We seek partnerships that help us test, improve and validate our systems.

Development roadmap

FROM LAB TO IMPACT

  1. Document and benchmark MOSS/KYVRAEL capabilities.
  2. Build repeatable demonstrations and practical customer pilots.
  3. Secure owned GPU workstations and server infrastructure.
  4. Expand research partnerships and hardware validation.
